# AML/CTF Training Pack — Outline &amp; Content

**{{FIRM_LEGAL_NAME}} · Annexure to the AML/CTF Program**
Version {{VERSION}} · Approved {{APPROVAL_DATE}}

> Four role-based training tracks covering the partner / lawyer / paralegal / admin populations. Each track is delivered initially on commencement (within 30 days), refreshed annually, and re-delivered whenever a material change to the program is approved.

---

## Track 1 — Partners / Principals (90 minutes)

### Learning objectives
By the end of the session, partners will be able to:
1. State the Firm's AUSTRAC enrolment status and which of the Firm's designated services trigger the AML/CTF Act.
2. Describe their personal accountability under the Act and the consequences of breach (civil penalty, criminal liability for officers).
3. Recognise the top red-flag indicators in their practice area.
4. Apply the escalation protocol to a worked example.
5. Articulate the role of the AMLCO and the limits of partner-level direction.

### Agenda
| Minutes | Topic | Method |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Why we're here: Tranche 2, deadlines, who is captured | Slide deck |
| 10–25 | Our service mix and which matters are designated services | Discussion |
| 25–45 | Personal accountability — AMLCO appointment, partner liability, tipping-off | Slide deck + case study |
| 45–65 | Red flags by practice area | Worked scenarios |
| 65–80 | Escalation walkthrough | Tabletop exercise |
| 80–90 | Q&amp;A, sign attestation | Form |

### Materials
- Slide deck (Annexure K1)
- Two anonymised case studies (Annexure K2)
- Attestation form (Annexure K-Att)

## Track 2 — Lawyers / Fee-earners (60 minutes)

### Learning objectives
1. Identify, at intake, which matters within their workload are designated services.
2. Execute the Firm's CDD procedure (ID, beneficial owners, source of funds, screening).
3. Recognise red flags during a matter and document them.
4. Apply the escalation protocol — what to do, what NOT to do (tipping-off).
5. Operate the Firm's recordkeeping requirements for AML/CTF.

### Agenda
| Minutes | Topic | Method |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Tranche 2 in two slides | Slide deck |
| 10–25 | The intake workflow — live walkthrough of CDD inside the matter system | Demo |
| 25–40 | Red flags by practice area | Worked scenarios |
| 40–55 | Escalation walkthrough + tipping-off | Tabletop exercise |
| 55–60 | Q&amp;A, sign attestation | Form |

### Materials
- Slide deck (Annexure K3)
- Live demo on the DMS / matter management system
- Attestation form

---

## Track 3 — Paralegals / Clerks (45 minutes)

### Learning objectives
1. Operate the initial intake CDD steps (ID collection, beneficial owner identification, screening).
2. Identify what counts as a CDD red flag at intake and how to flag it to the supervising fee-earner.
3. Understand the recordkeeping requirement and where AML/CTF artefacts are stored.
4. Understand tipping-off — what they can and cannot say to a client about an escalation.

### Agenda
| Minutes | Topic | Method |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Why we do this (90-second version) | Slide |
| 5–25 | Intake demo — the buttons to click in the DMS | Demo |
| 25–35 | What to flag and to whom | Worked examples |
| 35–40 | Tipping-off | Slide + Q&amp;A |
| 40–45 | Sign attestation | Form |

### Materials
- Quick-reference intake checklist (laminated, one-page)
- Attestation form

---

## Track 4 — Admin &amp; Reception (30 minutes)

### Learning objectives
1. Know what the Firm's AML/CTF program is and where to find it.
2. Recognise the categories of staff who should be told if a client asks an unusual question about the Firm's reporting obligations.
3. Understand tipping-off and confidentiality.

### Agenda
| Minutes | Topic | Method |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | What is AML/CTF and why we have a program | Conversation |
| 10–20 | What to do if a client asks an unusual question | Worked examples |
| 20–25 | Confidentiality and tipping-off | Slide |
| 25–30 | Q&amp;A, sign attestation | Form |

### Materials
- One-page handout
- Attestation form

## Annual refresher template (all tracks · 30 minutes)

For tracks that have completed the initial training within the last 12 months:

2. Two new red-flag scenarios from the past year (15 min)
3. AMLCO report — what we've reported to AUSTRAC, anonymised (5 min)
4. Open Q&amp;A (5 min)
