# How to use the Lawgistics Academy, for the firm's interns

`index.html` is the source of a 60 second portrait video for the firm's
interns, served at `/video/how-to-use-interns.mp4`. Interns are not
litigation trainees: they join by an invitation link, use the academy as
homework whenever it suits them, and have no morning rounds, so none of that
is in it. The trainees have their own video in `../how-to-use/`.

The tour and Learn screens were captured from a Malaysian learner (not
staff, not a trainee) on the mock backend; the score screen carries sample
data and is marked "Example". Joining, the diagnostic, training and a
question are drawn, the question with blank lines so no legal content
appears that a lawyer has not signed off.

`../../public/video/how-to-use-interns.mp4`.
