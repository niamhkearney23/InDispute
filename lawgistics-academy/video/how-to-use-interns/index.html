How to use the

Lawgistics Academy

Your homework, whenever it suits you.

1Join with your link

Your supervisor sends you a link. It is yours alone and works once.

Choose a password and tap Create my account.

2Take the tour

A quick tour shows what each part is and where to find it.

3The diagnostic

About 30 questions. There is no pass mark. It shows where you are starting from.

4Train when it suits you

A few minutes at a time, from Today.

What you get wrong comes back until you have it.

5Answering

Pick an answer, then say how sure you are. Be honest.

You see straight away whether you were right, and why.

6Learn

Modules teach for a few minutes, then ask. Each has a finishing line.

7Your score

It starts at 100%. Only a wrong answer brings it down.
