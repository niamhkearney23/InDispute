## What we collect

- **Your account:** name, email address, country (Malaysia or Australia),
  password (stored only as a one-way hash by our sign-in provider), and for
  staff the code app linked for two-step sign-in.
- **Your profile, if you add them:** a photo or a cartoon you build, your
  timezone, career stage and goals.
- **Your training:** every answer you give, when, and whether it was right;
  your scores, streaks and progress; the diagnostic; homework you tick.
- **Your work:** documents and recordings you hand in, matter attempts
  (including spoken explanations you record), marks and comments from
  coaches, and messages on the work board.
- **Supervision records:** the joining checklist, decisions a supervisor
  records about you, certification register entries a coach makes about
  your work, and certificates issued to you.
- **If the tutor is switched on:** what you type to the tutor and its replies.
- **Technical:** sign-in cookies that keep you signed in, and the server logs
  our hosting provider keeps (address, browser, time) for security.

We ask you never to put a client's name or any detail that identifies a
client in anything you hand in, upload or type. Each upload asks you to
confirm this.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself on the Account page, or ask
**[contact]**. When an account is deleted:

- you are signed out everywhere and can no longer sign in;
- your name, email address, photo and cartoon, profile details, tutor
  conversations and recordings are deleted;
- your work-board messages are removed, except any a supervisor marked as
  needed for supervision;
- the firm keeps your training and work records (answers, submissions and
  their files, marks, certificates, supervision decisions) because it relies
  on them. They are no longer linked to your name, **but they may still
  identify you**, for example through what you wrote or uploaded, or a
  certification entry a coach made. An administrator reviews them: each
  certification record is either stripped of identifying details (your
  name, notes and the matter reference; grades and dates stay) or kept with
  a recorded reason, and messages that mention you can be removed.

If any part of the deletion cannot finish straight away (for example, a
file cannot be removed), you are told, and it is retried until it is done.
