## 1. What the academy is

The academy is a training tool run by **[firm's legal name]** ("the firm")
for its trainees, interns, paralegals and staff, using software provided by
**[Lawgistics / the owner's entity]**.

**It is training, not legal advice.** Questions, lessons, court guides,
daily briefs and matters are written to teach. Nothing in the academy is
legal advice, and nothing in it may be relied on for a real matter. Always
check the current law, rules and practice directions, and ask your
supervising lawyer.

**What has been checked.** Learners see only content a named, qualified
lawyer has signed off, and the record of who signed it off is kept. A
sign-off is a careful review, not a guarantee: the law changes, and mistakes
are possible. If you think something is wrong, tell **[contact]** or flag it
to your coach.

**Levels and certificates.** Levels, scores and streaks are game features.
A certificate of completion records that you finished the firm's training
programme. None of them is a professional qualification, a practising
certificate, admission, or evidence of competence for any regulator.

## 2. Your account

- Use your own account only, and keep your password private. Staff must use
  two-step sign-in.
- Give accurate details. Trainee status and firm access codes are confirmed
  by a coach or administrator before they take effect.
- Tell **[contact]** straight away if you think someone else has used your
  account.

## 3. What you must not do

- Put a client's name, or anything that identifies a client or a real
  matter, in anything you write, upload or record. Uploads ask you to
  confirm this each time.
- Upload anything you do not have the right to share, or anything unlawful,
  offensive or harmful.
- Try to see other people's information, get around the academy's security,
  test it without written permission, or overload it.
- Copy, sell or publish the academy's content outside your training without
  permission.
- Use the AI features (if switched on) to produce work you present as legal
  advice, or enter confidential information into them.

## 4. AI features

If the firm switches them on, some features use an AI service: the tutor,
follow-up questions on matters, and time estimates for work. AI replies can
be wrong. The tutor is built never to state law and to send legal questions
to the lessons or a coach; it does not mark you, except where it marks
against an answer a lawyer has signed off. What you type is sent to the AI
provider as described in the privacy notice.

## 5. Your work and our content

You keep the rights in the work you hand in. You allow the firm to store,
review, mark and keep it as part of your training and supervision records,
as described in the privacy notice. The academy's content and software
belong to **[Lawgistics / the owner's entity]** and **[the firm, for its own
documents]**; you may use them for your own training while you have an
account.

## 6. Ending your use

notice explains what is deleted and what the firm keeps). The firm may
suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, or when you leave the
firm or the programme ends, **[with notice where reasonable]**.
