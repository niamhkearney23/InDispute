Built to make law work for businesses

Most Malaysian SMEs do not avoid legal documentation because they do not value it. They avoid it because the process is slow, the cost is unpredictable, and the language is written for lawyers rather than for the people running the business.

Lawgistics exists to remove all three obstacles. Our templates are drafted by practising Malaysian lawyers, structured around guided questions in plain English, and priced so that documenting a deal properly is never the expensive option.
