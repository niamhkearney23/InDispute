No systems

What we offer

An opportunity to launch your independent legal practice.

Support as you begin your journey to establish your own practice, so you can register and start without the usual standing start.

Financial assistance

An investment to cover your Bar Council fees and insurance, so you can register as an independent lawyer without delay.

Technology support

A laptop equipped with the practice management software a law firm actually runs on.

Workspace

Shared office space within a group practice for 100 weeks, a professional setting to meet clients and manage matters.

Initial work value

RM 1,000 per week, repayable in the form of work over four years. Repayment is spread into manageable contributions rather than a lump sum hanging over you.

Mentorship

One hour every week: thirty minutes on technical legal skills, thirty minutes on business development.

Your commitment

In return, you agree to pay 10% of your gross income, so both sides share in the practice as it grows.
