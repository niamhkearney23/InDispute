How the training works

Two halves, marked in two different places.

This page describes the training. Only one of the two halves is marked here.

Advocacy, on this site

Read a problem, argue it out loud, and get structured feedback on your reasoning, your structure and how you present. No account, nothing to install, and it is free while Lawgistics is in beta.

Start an exercise →

Questions, in the app

A short diagnostic works out what you already know, then you get asked about what you do not. Answers are checked on the server rather than in your browser, and anything you get wrong comes back later until it stops being wrong. That needs an account, because the point of it is the record.
