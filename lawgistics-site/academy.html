For firms

This page is the demonstration.

Everything above is the product running, not a mockup of it. What you are reading is the same software a firm is given, with the same content and the same refusal to publish anything a named person has not signed off. Under your firm's name it would look like this, with your crest and your colour on it.

Your name on it

The name, the wordmark, the tagline and the accent colour are settings, not a fork. One firm, one deployment, your branding throughout. Your people never see ours.

Your induction, in your words

Your welcome, your AI policy and your joining checklist sit beside the training and never mix with it. An acknowledgement is pinned to the version somebody read, so republishing a policy puts it back in front of everyone.

A record that holds up

Nothing here concludes that a person is ready. A named supervisor decides, and the record keeps their name, the date, and how many items were still outstanding when they decided. Mistakes are corrected by recording a correction, never by deleting.

What we will not claim. This is one firm per deployment, not a platform serving many firms from one place. Every question carries its jurisdiction, and the interface will not publish one that has no named sign-off recorded against it. The exception is the starter content, which ships already published so that a new installation is not empty, and which the review queue counts and shows you until a person has been through it. The Malaysian bank is not even allowed that: it cannot publish itself at all, and goes out one signed-off item at a time.
