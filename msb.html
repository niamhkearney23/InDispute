FAQ

Common questions.

Our bank is asking for an independent review. Will yours be accepted? +

Generally yes. We write our reports to the standard the major Australian banks expect from MSB customers — explicit independence declaration, sample-based testing, prioritised remediation plan. We can speak with your bank's financial-crime team directly if helpful.

We're a sub-agent in a network. Do we need our own program? +

It depends on whether you're enrolled in your own right or operating under the principal's enrolment. Either way, the principal will expect you to have local procedures aligned to their program. We can build the local-procedures layer for you in a couple of weeks.

Cash businesses worry us. Should we just stop accepting cash? +

Not necessarily. Cash is allowed; it just requires controls proportionate to the risk. We build a cash-policy framework with threshold rules, EDD triggers, and refusal procedures. You get to keep serving your customer base while documenting why each transaction is reasonable.
