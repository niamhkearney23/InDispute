# Partner outreach — sequence and sourcing

**Goal:** secure 5–8 introductory calls with senior AML practitioners over the next 4 weeks. Pick one to move forward with into a letter of intent within 8 weeks.

**Cadence:** 3 touches over 14 days. Slower than direct-SME outreach; senior practitioners read carefully.

**Voice:** peer-to-peer, specific, honest. Do not oversell. The pitch is "I've built the chassis, I need a delivery partner, here's the honest version."

---

## Sourcing — practical steps

### LinkedIn (primary)
Search filters:
- Title: `Senior Manager` OR `Director` OR `Partner` OR `Principal`
- Industry: Business Consulting and Services / Financial Services
- Company: PwC OR EY OR Deloitte OR KPMG OR Grant Thornton OR BDO OR Pitcher Partners OR RSM OR Findex
- Location: Greater Melbourne Area (also Sydney as fallback)
- Keywords in bio: "AML" OR "financial crime" OR "AUSTRAC" OR "Tranche 2" OR "CAMS"

Build a list of 30-40 candidates ranked by:
1. Tranche 2 thought leadership (LinkedIn posts, articles)
2. Tenure in current role (2-7 years = peak leaving window)
3. Mutual connections you have
4. Profile activity (active posters are reachable)

### AUSTRAC / regulator alumni
LinkedIn: past company "AUSTRAC", "ASIC", "Australian Federal Police", "APRA". Cross-reference with current role in private practice or consulting.

### ACAMS Australia chapter
Members visible on ACAMS site. Chapter committee members are particularly senior and reachable.

### Conference speakers (last 24 months)
- ACAMS ANZ Conference
- AFC Summit (Anti-Financial Crime Summit Australia)
- RegTech Association events
- AUSTRAC Innovation Hub events

Speakers are public-facing and used to being approached.

### Existing AML boutiques
Search AUSTRAC reporting entity register for businesses registered as AML/CTF consultants. Cross-reference with LinkedIn for principals.

### Warm intros (use before cold)
Anyone in your network at:
- The Big 4 (junior-mid managers can route)
- A Tier 1 / Tier 2 bank's financial crime function
- A regulated payments / fintech firm
- A boutique law firm's compliance partner

## Touch 2 — Day 6 — Specific hook

**Subject:** Re: productised AML practice — what's on the table

Hi {{FIRST_NAME}},

Following on from last week. To make this concrete, the working version of the commercial structure I'd start with:

- Co-founder equity: 35–40%, four-year vest, 12-month cliff
- Salary while pre-revenue: drawn equally between us at whatever rate the bank account supports
- I underwrite the first 6 months of operating costs (insurance, AUSTRAC, software)
- You own all delivery; I own all GTM
- Both sign every quote going out, until you're comfortable I won't quote things you can't deliver

If those numbers are wrong by a wide margin I'd want to know what would be right.

The full pitch is here: {{PITCH_URL}}

{{SENDER_NAME}}

## Reply playbook

| Reply pattern | Response |
|---|---|
| "Interested — let's talk." | Confirm a time. Send the pitch doc + the site directory as pre-read. |
| "Send more info." | Send pitch doc (PDF rendered from `partner-pitch.html`). Note: 30-min call is the next step, not more docs. |
| "I'm staying where I am." | Ask: "Understood. Anyone in your network who might be in a different position right now?" |
| "I'd want more equity." | Don't argue on email. "Worth discussing live — happy to talk through what each side is contributing." |
| "What's your background?" | Be honest. Lead with what you've built; acknowledge you're not the AML expert and that's why you're looking. |
| "Why should I trust this is real?" | Send the site URL, the directory, the Big 4 pitch HTML. Offer references for the GTM work. |
| Out of office / no reply | Wait 30 days, re-approach with a new hook (a Tranche 2 development, a delivered first client). |

---

## What to send when they ask for pre-reading

1. `partner-pitch.html` (rendered to PDF — the polished pitch)
2. The site URL — let them poke around
3. The directory URL — show them every artefact
4. The Big 4 pitch HTML — show that the channel work is real

Do NOT send the program template until you're in commercial discussions under NDA. That's the IP that has to stay with the practice.

## Sequencing across personas

Don't run all three personas in parallel. Start with Persona 1 (Big 4 leavers) — largest pool, most likely fit, fastest sourcing. If 4 weeks of Persona 1 outreach doesn't land a fit, move to Persona 2 (ex-regulator). Persona 3 (existing boutique consultants) is structurally harder because they have their own thing — leave for last.
