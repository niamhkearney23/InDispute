The 100-night trial

You cannot tell in a showroom

Eight minutes lying on a bed in a bright shop tells you almost nothing. It takes about three weeks for your body to settle onto a new mattress — so that is how long we think you should get.

  1. Order and we deliver freeUsually two to five working days, and we take the old mattress away.
  2. Sleep on it for at least 21 nightsThe first week can feel strange. That is normal, and it passes.
  3. Keep it, swap it, or send it backAny time inside 100 nights. Returned beds are donated or recycled, never resold as new.
