FAQ

Questions we get every week

Can I try a mattress before buying?
Yes — all three are made up on real bases in the Fitzroy shop, Thursday to Sunday. Bring your own pillow if you want a fair test. That said, twenty minutes in a shop tells you far less than three weeks at home, which is what the trial is for.
How firm is "medium", really?
Roughly a 6 out of 10, where 1 is a beanbag and 10 is a floor. Firmness is also relative to your weight — the same bed reads a step softer for someone heavier. There is a full explanation in the firmness guide.
Do you take the old mattress away?
Yes, free, at the same delivery. It gets dismantled and recycled — steel to scrap, foam to carpet underlay, timber chipped. We do not resell trial returns as new, ever.
What if two of us disagree about firmness?
If the weight difference is under about 20 kg, a medium hybrid usually satisfies both. Beyond that, the honest answer is a split-firmness king or two long singles zipped together. Email us and we will price it up either way.
Do you price match?
Not really — most of what we sell is not stocked elsewhere. If you find an identical item cheaper at an authorised Australian retailer, send us the link and we will either match it or tell you plainly to buy it there.
Is there a sale coming?
We do two a year, in June and late November, at around 15% off. If one is within a fortnight and you email to ask, we will tell you — waiting a week to save $300 seems reasonable to us.
