One box, made properly

Sleep Shop is a small studio in Fitzroy. We make one thing, which means we get to be unreasonable about all eight pieces in it.

Why only one box

Gift shops solve the wrong problem. They give you forty things and leave you to assemble a gift out of them, which is the part that actually takes the effort — working out whether the candle goes with the tea, whether it looks mean with only three items in it, whether the whole thing reads as considered or as panic-bought at 6pm.

So we did that part. One box, eight pieces, tested against each other rather than chosen from a catalogue. The candle is unscented because there is already lavender in the box. The scrunchie is cut from the pillowcase offcuts so the colour matches exactly. Those decisions are the product.

It also means we can be particular about supply. Eight things is a small enough list to know where every one of them comes from, and to notice immediately when something changes.

Materials

What things are made of

We publish the fibre, the weight and the maker for everything in the box. If a supplier will not tell us, we do not use them.

Silk

Grade 6A mulberry silk at 22 momme for the eye mask, pillowcase and scrunchie, all cut from the same run. Heavy enough to drape and to survive being washed properly, rather than the 16-momme silk most gift sets use.

Wool and wax

Socks knitted in Melbourne from 19.5-micron Australian merino, traceable to the growing region. The candle is pure beeswax with a cotton wick — no paraffin, no soy blend, no fragrance oil.

The box and the card

Rigid board wrapped in uncoated paper, made in Victoria, sturdy enough to be reused for something else. The card is letterpress on cotton stock, printed two suburbs away.

The bench

How a box gets packed

Everything is packed on one bench, in the same order every time: tissue, pillowcase, mask, scrunchie, socks, then the four small things along the front so nothing rattles. The card is written last, once, in pen — there is no draft copy.

Two of us pack, and on a normal weekday we get through about forty boxes. If you order before 2pm your box is very likely one of that day's forty.

We do not have a shopfront. There is a studio address in Fitzroy and you are welcome to collect from it by appointment, which saves you the delivery wait if you are local and have left it late.

Straight answer

We sell a gift, not an outcome

We are careful about what we claim. This is a box of well-made things for the end of a day — silk, wool, beeswax, tea, a blank journal. We do not make claims about what it will do for anyone, because that is not something a box can promise and we would rather not pretend otherwise.
