A small shop, on purpose

Hush started in 2016 in a Fitzroy shopfront that used to be a laundromat. We sell about twenty things. Every one of them has been slept on by someone who works here, for at least a month, before it went on the shelf.

Why so few products?

Because a wall of forty mattresses is not choice, it is abdication. Somebody has to do the work of narrowing it down, and if the shop will not, the customer has to — usually while lying awkwardly on a display bed with a salesperson watching.

So we did the narrowing. Three mattresses, because there are really only three useful answers: springs with foam on top, all foam, and latex. Three pillows, because pillow choice is mostly a question of how tall you need it. And then the things around the bed that turn out to matter more than people expect — sheets that breathe, a curtain that actually blocks light, a lamp that wakes you gently.

Nobody here works on commission. There is no incentive to move you up a bracket, and plenty of days we talk someone out of a mattress and into a $129 pillow.

Materials

What things are made of

We publish the fibre, the certification and the country for everything we sell. If a supplier will not tell us, we do not stock it.

Foam and latex

All polyurethane foam is CertiPUR certified for emissions and content. Our latex is natural Dunlop, tapped rather than synthesised. No mattress we sell contains a fibreglass fire sock — we use a wool barrier instead, which costs more and does not shed.

Fibres

Cotton is long-staple and OEKO-TEX certified. Linen is certified European flax. Wool is Australian and traceable to the growing region. Down is RDS certified, which means no live plucking and no force feeding.

What we will not stock

Copper-infused anything, "ionised" fabrics, gravity blankets making medical claims, and mattresses that arrive with a two-year warranty and a five-year lifespan. All of it sells well. None of it works.

The fine print

The 100-night trial, in full

It applies to every mattress and every pillow. It starts the day the item is delivered, not the day you ordered it.

  • Sleep on it for at least 21 nights before deciding. Bodies adapt slowly, and almost every return we accept inside three weeks is a body that had not settled yet.
  • Any time before night 100, email or call. We arrange collection at no cost to you, anywhere in Australia.
  • Full refund to the original payment method, usually within five working days of collection. No restocking fee, no "condition assessment".
  • You do not need the box. You do not need the original packaging at all.
  • One trial return per household per product category, which is the only rule here that exists because somebody abused it.

Returned mattresses are never resold as new. They go to charity partners where the condition allows, and to a materials recycler where it does not.

Getting it to you

Delivery and returns

Delivery

  • Free Australia-wide on orders over $99. Below that it is a flat $12.
  • Metro capital cities: two to five working days. Regional: up to eight.
  • Mattresses and bed-sized items are delivered by a two-person team with a booked two-hour window, and we take your old mattress away at the same time.
  • We will carry it upstairs. Tell us at checkout if there is no lift.

Returns on everything else

  • Unopened bedding, sleepwear and accessories: 30 days, refunded in full.
  • Opened bedding: we cannot resell it, so we offer store credit and donate the item.
  • Faulty goods: replaced or refunded at your choice, whatever the timeframe, in line with Australian Consumer Law. Your rights there sit above anything on this page.
  • Return postage on faults is always ours.

What we do with the old ones

Since 2019 we have kept a bit over 4,000 mattresses out of landfill. Steel goes to scrap, foam is shredded for carpet underlay, and timber is chipped. It costs us about $38 a bed and we think that is a reasonable price.
