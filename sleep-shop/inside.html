What is inside

Four pieces, the ritual card, and your message. Everything in the box had to earn its place, and plenty of things did not make it. Here is what is in there and why, including what each one is made of.

05 / Letterpress

The ritual card

A short evening ritual, letterpressed on cotton board, sitting on top of the tissue. The same card is in every box. It is not instructions, it is an invitation: five small steps for the first night, in the order that makes each one easier to start.

06 / In your hand, then ours

Your message

Up to 250 characters, typed at checkout and written onto a card by hand before the lid goes on. It is the only paper in the box apart from the ritual card. No invoice, no receipt, no pricing anywhere on the parcel.

And that is all of it

Four pieces, one ritual

No filler, no sachets, nothing added to make the box look fuller than it is. Four things that work together, the ritual card on top, and your message written by hand, in a box that is worth keeping.
