Sending it straight to them

Most of our boxes never touch the hands of the person who bought them. That is what the whole thing is built around.

How it works

Four steps, one of them yours

    Start a box

    The card

    Written by hand, not printed

    Up to 220 characters

    Roughly three lines on a letterpress card. Longer than that and the handwriting stops being legible, which defeats the point.

    We write it as you type it

    Punctuation, line breaks, the lot. We will quietly fix an obvious typo in a name, and leave everything else exactly as you wrote it.

    Nothing else goes in

    No invoice, no receipt, no pricing on the outside of the parcel. The card is the only paper in the box.

    Delivery

    Getting it there

    • Free Australia-wide on every box. Express is $12 at checkout.
    • Ordered before 2pm on a weekday, it leaves Melbourne the same afternoon.
    • Metro capitals: two to four working days. Regional: up to eight.
    • Sending it to a workplace is fine — tell us the company name in the address and we will put it on the label rather than on the card.
    • We do not ship outside Australia yet. If you ask, we will tell you honestly when that changes rather than take the order.

    Returns

    If it is not right

    • Unopened boxes: 30 days, refunded in full, return postage on us.
    • Opened boxes cannot be resold, so we cannot take those back — but if something arrives damaged or a piece is missing, we replace the whole box, not the piece.
    • Wrong address, or you changed your mind before it went out? Email within an hour and we will catch it on the packing bench.
    • Faults are covered under Australian Consumer Law whatever the timeframe, and those rights sit above anything written on this page.

    More than one

    Sending several

    Different messages, one order

    Add a box, write the message, then add another. Each box in your cart keeps its own message and its own ribbon, and they all ship separately to whoever you nominate at checkout.

    Ten or more

    Email us before you order. We will confirm we can pack them in the timeframe you need, and for a run that size we can leave the ribbon off and use a plain band if the boxes are going out under a company name.
