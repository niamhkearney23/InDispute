FORM TP-27WHAT GUESTS SEE

What guests see

The invitation comes in slowly, “Opening Statement” letter by letter, then the red PARTY APPROVED chop comes down as they scroll to it. Below it is a reply slip they tear off and file; their signature writes itself.

·What they get after filing

Coming: “Motion granted, Mathew. You and your plus one are on the record. See you on Friday, 8 January, from 6:30 PM.”

Not coming: “Noted for the record, Mathew. You will be missed.”

If something is missing, the slip asks for it (name, firm or organization, who invited them, yes or no, email) and does not send until it is complete.

·When you share the link

This picture shows in WhatsApp and iMessage when the invitation link is sent, with the title “You’re invited · Opening Statement 2027”.

FORM TP-27SENDING TO 150 GUESTS

Sending to 150 guests

Pick one way, or mix them: personal links for close guests, the shared link for a group chat.

FASTEST · ONE MESSAGE

One link in a group or broadcast list

Post invitationtp2027.netlify.app once. Everyone states their name on the way in, so the greeting is personal and you can still see who opened it.

WhatsApp broadcast lists only reach people who have your number saved. A WhatsApp group shows everyone’s number to everyone.

MOST PERSONAL · ABOUT 15 TO 20 MINUTES

Personal links by WhatsApp, text or email

  1. Open the guest list page and paste your guests, one per line. Add a mobile number or email after a comma if you have it, or paste straight from a spreadsheet.
  2. Check the country for numbers starting with 0 (set to Australia, +61; change it if needed), then press Create links.
  3. For each guest, tap WhatsApp, Text or Email. The message opens already written; you only press send. Each guest is ticked off as you go.
ONE CLICK · NEEDS EMAIL ADDRESSES

Email, sent for you

Give Claude the guests’ names and email addresses. Claude drafts one email for you to approve, then sends every guest their own personal link from your email.

02Everyone at a glance

In the sheet, open each tab and choose File → Download → Comma-separated values (.csv). Drop both files onto the guest list page. Every guest then shows Not sent, Sent, not opened, Opened, Coming +1 or Not coming, with totals and filters, so you can see who still needs a nudge.

03Good to know

  • An open is recorded once per guest on each device they use.
  • The WhatsApp link preview does not count as an open.
  • Guests on the shared link appear under the name they typed.
  • Your guest list (names, numbers, ticks) stays on your own device.

·Changing things later

  • Venue confirmed, a new time or new wording: ask Claude, then upload the new zip on the Deploys tab. The link stays the same and everyone sees the update.
  • If you ever edit the sheet’s Apps Script, use Deploy → Manage deployments → Edit → New version, so its web address does not change.

·If something looks off

  • Replies are not appearing in the sheet: in Apps Script, check the deployment is a Web app with access set to “Anyone”, then send a test reply. Ask Claude if it still does not appear.
