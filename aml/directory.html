Strategic pivot · May 2026

After InfoTrack launched the free, Grant-Thornton-backed AML/CTF Compliance Centre integrated into PMS, the strategy was re-scoped. New positioning: the human compliance layer above any platform (outsourced AMLCO, independent reviews) + sectors no platform serves (TCSPs, DPMS, gambling, MSB, crypto, bullion). Full reasoning in competitive-memo.md.
