FAQ

Common questions from crypto operators.

We use Chainalysis. Isn't that enough? +

No. Chainalysis (or TRM, Elliptic) gives you on-chain risk scoring — that's a transaction monitoring input, not a program. You still need the risk assessment, AMLCO, CDD procedures, decision logs, training, reporting and independent review wrapping around the tool.

Travel Rule — what does that actually require? +

FATF Recommendation 16 (Travel Rule) requires originator and beneficiary information to travel with virtual asset transfers between regulated entities. Australia has implemented a version of this. The mechanics depend on your counterparties and the protocols you use. We map your transfer flows and tell you exactly what to implement.

We're pre-revenue. Do we really need a real program? +

If you're providing a designated service to even one customer, yes. Most banks and partners won't engage without it; investors increasingly demand it as part of diligence. The cheapest path is a defensible program from day one.
