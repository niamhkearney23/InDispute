The pitch in three sentences

I've built a productised AML/CTF compliance practice for boutique professional firms — five sector landing pages, a unified-program template, a 12-point readiness diagnostic, three engagement tiers, a Big 4 channel pitch, full outreach machinery. What's missing is you. I'm looking for one senior AML practitioner to come in as a co-founder or consulting principal and own the delivery side from day one.

Why this is worth 30 minutes

Most AML practitioners who go independent spend their first 12 months building what you've already lived through at a Big 4 or Tier 2: brand, website, productised offering, pricing, marketing, lead gen. Then they discover selling AML services to boutique firms is a different sales problem from the ADI / super / ASX engagements they're used to. I've done that 12 months of work in the wrong order — built the GTM first, deferred delivery. The result is a working chassis that needs a delivery practitioner inside it.

The honest version of why I'm doing it this way

I've watched the Tranche 2 wave coming. The market is real and time-bound. I have the GTM instincts and the willingness to build, but I don't have the regulatory depth to credibly deliver Tier 2 and Tier 3 engagements alone. Pretending otherwise is bad for clients, bad for the brand, and exposes both sides to risk. I'd rather give up a substantial slice of the business to someone who knows what they're doing than be the kind of consultant who runs ahead of their actual expertise.
