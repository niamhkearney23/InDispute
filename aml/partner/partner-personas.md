# Target partner personas

Three realistic profiles to target. Listed in priority order — try Persona 1 first, then 2, then 3.

---

## Persona 1 — The Big 4 senior leaver

**Profile**
- Senior Manager or Director at PwC, EY, Deloitte or KPMG financial crime / forensic / regulatory practice
- 8-15 years post-qualification
- CAMS or equivalent
- Has delivered AML/CTF engagements to ADIs, super, payments, insurers
- Australian, Melbourne or Sydney based (Melbourne strong preference)

**Why they'd leave**
- Big 4 senior manager / director ceiling is real — partnership track is unrelenting and zero-sum.
- Tranche 2 has created a market they personally see but their firm can't profitably serve (their own SME inbound).
- Wants to control delivery quality rather than supervise a team of juniors doing it.
- Burned out on time recording, recovery, partner politics.
- Wants equity / upside rather than salary.

**Sourcing channels**
- LinkedIn search: "Senior Manager" OR "Director" + ("Financial Crime" OR "AML" OR "Regulatory Risk") + (PwC OR EY OR Deloitte OR KPMG) + Australia
- Filter by 2-7 years in current role (peak leaving window) and 1-2 prior job moves visible
- ACAMS Australia chapter member list and event speakers
- AFC Summit, RegTech Association events

**What they want to hear from you**
- The delivery accountability is theirs, not yours
- Genuine equity, not a sham
- That you'll bring sales pipeline, not just admin
- That you've done the GTM work they don't want to do

**What scares them off**
- "Founder of a startup" language — they want a practice, not a startup
- Anything that smells like reputational risk
- Vague commercial terms
- The sense that they'd be doing 100% of the work for 30% of the equity

## Persona 2 — The ex-AUSTRAC / regulator

**Profile**
- 5-15 years at AUSTRAC, ASIC, AFP, or APRA in a financial-crime-adjacent role
- May have already left to go independent or to industry
- Deep regulator-side knowledge of what good looks like
- Strong public-sector network

**Why they'd partner**
- Regulator-side comp is capped; private practice income is significant uplift.
- Wants to apply expertise rather than supervise enforcement.
- The "AUSTRAC alumni" branding is genuinely valuable to clients.

**Sourcing channels**
- LinkedIn: "AUSTRAC" in past employers + Australia
- AICD (Australian Institute of Company Directors) network
- AFC Summit / ACAMS speaker list
- People who recently published on Tranche 2 from ex-regulator perspective

**What they want to hear**
- That their regulator credentials will be front and centre in the brand
- That their network is going to be respected, not just exploited
- That the practice will be defensible, not aggressive

**What scares them off**
- Pricing aggressively (looks like cowboy operation)
- Anything that compromises their reputation
- A founder who treats them as "the AML person" rather than a partner

## Persona 3 — The boutique AML consultant already independent

**Profile**
- Already running an independent AML practice, probably 1-3 people
- 10-25 years in financial crime
- Profitable but capacity-constrained
- Probably no website beyond a basic placeholder
- Probably overweight on Tier 1 (templates) work, light on Tier 3

**Why they'd partner**
- They have delivery expertise; they don't have GTM / scaling.
- Tranche 2 has tripled their inbound but they can only serve a fraction.
- Joining ATTICUS could mean either selling their practice and rolling in, or running them in parallel (with referrals flowing both ways).

**Sourcing channels**
- AUSTRAC's public consultant directory / reporting entity register
- LinkedIn: "AML consultant" + Australia + Self-Employed or 1-10 employees
- ACAMS chapter, IRC (International Regulatory Compliance) networks
- Referrals from clients who've previously engaged smaller consultancies

**What they want to hear**
- That ATTICUS isn't going to be a competitor; it's the upgrade path
- That their existing clients are protected
- That their style of delivery is respected (not "we'll teach you how we do it")

**What scares them off**
- Looking like a corporate roll-up
- Loss of professional identity
- Equity that doesn't reflect what they're contributing

---

## Disqualifiers (all personas)

Skip if:
- They're not CAMS certified or equivalent and aren't willing to be within 6 months.
- They've never written an AML program for a real client.
- They're a great salesperson but light on regulatory depth (we already have the GTM).
- They want to be the founder/CEO — that's not what this is.
- They want to start a different kind of AML business (e.g. transaction monitoring tech, sanctions screening SaaS). Different shape.
