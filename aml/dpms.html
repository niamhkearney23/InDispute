The deadline

7 weeks until 1 July 2026.

Enrolment with AUSTRAC was due 29 March. Full program obligations commence 1 July 2026.

The scope

Cash transactions are the trigger.

Buying or selling precious metals and stones in transactions involving A$10,000 or more in cash is a designated service. Structuring (splitting transactions to avoid the threshold) is a separate reporting obligation.

The gap

There's no industry-body program.

Unlike lawyers and accountants, DPMS has no professional body publishing a sector program. Most shops are starting from zero, and most don't yet realise.

FAQ

Common questions from owners.

Most of our transactions are well below $10,000 in cash. Are we still in? +

Probably yes. The threshold is a reporting trigger (TTR), not an enrolment trigger. If you ever do an above-threshold transaction — or if your customers split transactions to stay below it — you need the program in place. AUSTRAC enrolment was due 29 March 2026 if you provide DPMS services at all.

We're a jeweller, not a bullion dealer. Does this apply? +

If you buy or sell precious metals or precious stones (including high-value rings, bracelets and loose diamonds) in cash transactions, yes. Pure costume jewellery without precious-metal content isn't captured. The readiness checklist will pin this down.

What about scrap-gold buying? +

Scrap-gold buying is one of the highest-flagged ML/TF risks in DPMS. Walk-in scrap with cash payouts attracts particular AUSTRAC attention. Your program needs explicit controls around it.

How much owner time does it actually take? +
