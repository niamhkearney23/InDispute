FAQ

Common questions from partners.

We only do conveyancing and a bit of estate work. Are we really in scope? +

Almost certainly yes. Buying or selling real estate for a client and managing client money are designated services. The readiness checklist will tell you in 8 minutes which of your matter types are captured.

Why a productised template instead of a bespoke build? +

Boutique firms have the same obligations as top-tier firms but a fraction of the resources. A productised program lets us reuse the structural work and spend our time on the parts that are genuinely firm-specific — your service mix, your matter intake, your AMLCO model.

How much partner time does it actually take? +

For Done-With-You: a 60-minute scoping call, a 90-minute risk workshop, and roughly two hours reviewing the draft. We can usually go live within a partner-day of effort spread over two weeks.

Who's behind this? +

ATTICUS is a Melbourne practice focused exclusively on AML/CTF for small and mid-sized professional firms.

Can you act as our external AMLCO? +

Yes — that's Tier 3. The AMLCO sits with us, reports to your board, and handles AUSTRAC interactions. You keep practising law.
