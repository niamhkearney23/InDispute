The deadline

From 1 July 2026, the AMLCO is personally on the hook.

Enrolment with AUSTRAC was due 29 March. Full program obligations start 1 July. Boards, partners and named officers carry the residual personal exposure.

The gap

Platforms automate workflow. They don't sign off.

Compliance platforms like InfoTrack do an excellent job of running CDD, holding records and producing the paperwork. But the Act requires judgement: forming suspicion, deciding when EDD is needed, signing the program. That's a person.

The risk

An AMLCO without time, training or independence is a liability.

Most boutique firms nominate a partner who has neither the bandwidth nor the AML background. AUSTRAC has already signalled it will look at AMLCO suitability in early reviews.

FAQ

Common questions.

We already use InfoTrack's Compliance Centre. Why do we need you? +

You may not. If your firm is low-risk, single-office, and one of your partners has the time and AML knowledge to be the AMLCO, the platform plus an in-house officer is probably enough. We help where it isn't — where the AMLCO is over-stretched or under-qualified, where the firm has international or trust-structuring exposure, or where an independent third party is needed for the periodic review. Our honest comparison page walks through the decision; take the readiness checklist for an answer specific to your firm.

Isn't InfoTrack's Compliance Centre free? +

The software is genuinely free. The searches are not. Every client onboarding requires VOI, PEP, sanctions, adverse-media and (often) beneficial-owner checks — and those are pay-per-use. The free-until-31-May-2026 offer applies only to verifying your firm's own staff, not your clients. For a firm doing ~200 onboardings/year, expect annual InfoTrack spend in the $2,000–$6,000 range depending on check mix. Still good value for what the platform does — just not "complimentary" in the literal sense.

Can a platform vendor do our independent review? +

No. The Rules require the reviewer to be independent of the operation of the program. A vendor reviewing the outputs of its own platform fails the independence test. The review must come from someone outside that supply chain.

What if our sector doesn't have a platform like InfoTrack? +

TCSPs, DPMS, gambling, MSB, digital currency exchanges and bullion dealers don't have an InfoTrack-equivalent. The full stack is still required — risk assessment, program, CDD workflow, training, AMLCO, reviews. We deliver all of it for those sectors directly.

Are you affiliated with a platform vendor? +

No. We are deliberately independent of every AML/CTF platform vendor. That independence is what lets us conduct credible reviews and give honest advice on whether a particular platform fits a particular firm.
