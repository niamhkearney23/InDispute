When the platform isn't enough

A platform alone is usually fine. Here's when it isn't.

Honest reading: most small low-risk firms can run on InfoTrack plus an internal AMLCO and never need us. These are the situations where that combination structurally fails:

Your nominal AMLCO doesn't have the time.

A partner billing 1,400 hours/year cannot also be a credible AMLCO. AUSTRAC has flagged that it will look at AMLCO suitability and bandwidth in early reviews. "Nominally appointed" is now a documented risk.

Nobody internal has AML experience.

Forming suspicion, deciding when EDD is genuinely triggered, drafting an SMR that AUSTRAC will accept — these are judgement calls that take 5+ years of AML practice to do well. Most boutique firms don't have anyone with that depth.

Your work touches non-trivial complexity.

International clients, multi-jurisdictional trusts, foreign investors, high-value off-the-plan property, family-office structures, layered ownership chains. None of these is exotic — but they push you into territory where a template program isn't defensible.

You need an independent review.

The Rules require periodic independent review of your program. The reviewer must be independent of the platform that runs your workflows. InfoTrack cannot do this for you, by structural design. We can.

AUSTRAC has written to you.

Information requests, audit notices, infringement notices, MOU breach concerns — all require human, legally-aware response. Platforms don't respond to regulators on your behalf.

You're the second engagement after a problem.

Bank de-risking, a near-miss SMR, an AUSTRAC information request, or a partner-level concern about personal liability — sometimes you need the named external AMLCO as protection.

FAQ

Common questions.

We're a small low-risk conveyancing practice. Do we really need an external AMLCO? +

Probably not. Honest read: InfoTrack plus a credible internal AMLCO covers a low-risk single-office conveyancing practice. We're not the right fit and we'll tell you so on the scoping call. The one thing you still need from someone like us is the periodic independent review — by design, your platform vendor can't do it.

Our managing partner is our AMLCO and she's overloaded. Is this our use case? +

Most likely yes. A managing partner cannot credibly be a full-billing partner, a firm leader, and a competent AMLCO simultaneously. Outsourcing the AMLCO function gives her time back, gets a qualified person on AUSTRAC's record, and moves the personal liability off her shoulders.

Our firm does international trust work. Is InfoTrack alone enough? +

No. International trust work is exactly the territory where template programs fail — beneficial-owner mapping, source-of-wealth verification, jurisdictional risk and EDD trigger judgement all need genuine AML practice depth. InfoTrack will help with workflow; it won't make the calls.

If we engage you, do we have to drop InfoTrack? +

No, and we'd usually recommend you keep it. Our retainer is designed to sit above your platform, not replace it. We are the AMLCO; InfoTrack runs the workflow underneath us.

Are you affiliated with InfoTrack? +

No. Independence from every AML/CTF platform vendor is a deliberate stance — it's what lets us conduct credible independent reviews and give honest advice on whether a particular platform fits a particular firm.
