FAQ

Common questions from TCSP principals.

We're a small corporate-services shop. Are we treated the same as a bank? +

Same statutory obligation under the Act, proportionate controls. A two-person TCSP doesn't need an enterprise-bank-grade transaction monitoring system, but it does need the same documented risk assessment, AMLCO, UBO procedures and reporting framework as a large firm.

Our incorporations are mostly online and high-volume. Does the same program work? +

Yes, with adjustments. High-volume, digitally-onboarded TCSP work needs an automated screening and beneficial-owner workflow plus risk-based exception handling. We design the program around your actual volume, not a generic template.

What about nominee director services specifically? +

Nominee director and trustee work is the highest-scrutiny TCSP service under FATF guidance. Expect AUSTRAC to ask first about your control-person verification, your understanding of the underlying beneficial owner, and your refusal-to-act procedures.

How much principal time does it actually take? +

For Done-With-You: a 60-minute scoping call, a 90-minute risk workshop, and roughly two hours reviewing the draft. We can usually go live within a principal-day of effort spread over two weeks.

Can you act as our external AMLCO? +

Yes — Tier 3. The AMLCO sits with us, reports to your principals, and handles AUSTRAC interactions. You keep running the corporate-services book.
