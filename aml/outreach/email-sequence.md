# Cold outreach — email sequence

**Goal:** 50 firms in the next 4 weeks → 5–10 booked engagements before 1 July 2026.
**Cadence:** 4 touches over 14 days. Stop on reply or after touch 4.
**Sender voice:** professional, peer-to-peer, no buzzwords, no "circling back."

---

## Subject line bank (rotate)

- `Quick question on your 1 July AML position`
- `{{FIRM_NAME}} — AML/CTF program for July?`
- `Boutique-firm AML/CTF program (8-min checklist inside)`
- `Are you the AMLCO at {{FIRM_NAME}}?`

Avoid: "compliance solution", "transform", "synergy", "circling back".

## Reply playbook

| Reply pattern | Response |
|---|---|
| "We have it covered." | Ask one question: "Out of interest — done in-house or with an external? Always interested in what good looks like at a similar firm." Park, set 6-month follow-up. |
| "Send more info." | Send the one-pager (link to landing page) + checklist link. Suggest 20-min call. |
| "Not in scope." | Respectfully challenge with specific service mix question (real estate, trust money, company formation). |
| "Pricing?" | Link to landing page pricing section. Offer 20-min scoping call to confirm tier. |
| "Talk to our practice manager." | Forward thread, cc the original partner. New touch 1 to PM. |
| Out of office / no reply by touch 4 | Move to nurture list — quarterly touch with regulatory update notes. |
