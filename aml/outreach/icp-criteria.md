# ICP — Boutique Melbourne law firms (AML/CTF launch)

## Why this ICP wins

Boutique firms have the same statutory obligation as top-tier firms but a fraction of the resources and zero appetite to build a dedicated compliance team. They are the most likely to (a) be confused, (b) have left it late, and (c) value a productised, fixed-fee solution.

## Hard filters (all must be true)

| Filter | Target |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Melbourne metropolitan (CBD, inner east, inner south, inner north) — start with Collins/Bourke St spine, South Yarra, Hawthorn, Carlton |
| **Headcount** | 3–25 fee-earners (excludes solo, excludes mid-tier and above) |
| **Practice area mix** | At least one of: property/conveyancing, trusts & estates, private client, corporate/commercial, family law (with property elements) |
| **Trust account** | Operates a statutory trust account |
| **Independence** | Not a branch of a national/international firm |
| **AUSTRAC enrolment** | Public AUSTRAC reporting entity register check — segment into (a) already enrolled, (b) not yet enrolled (hotter lead) |

## Soft signals (prioritise)

- Recent partner or hire announcement → triggers internal review of policies, easier to start a compliance conversation.
- Active LinkedIn presence by a managing partner or practice manager → contactable.
- Website mentions "trusted advisor", "family", "private client", "wealth" → high-value matters, AML risk is real.
- No mention of AML/CTF / compliance on website → likely under-prepared.
- Operates in property-heavy postcodes (Toorak, Brighton, Kew) → high-value real estate exposure.

## Disqualifiers

- Already publishes an AML/CTF program on their website (rare; means in-house capability).
- Pure litigation / criminal / personal injury practice with no designated services.
- Recently announced acquisition by a national firm (compliance handled centrally).

## Personas to contact

| Persona | Title patterns | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Managing Partner** | Managing Partner, Principal, Director | Strategic framing — risk, deadline, partnership liability |
| **Practice Manager / COO** | Practice Manager, General Manager, COO | Operational framing — workload, intake, training rollout |
| **Head of Risk / Compliance** (if exists) | Risk Partner, Compliance Lead | Peer framing — "what good looks like", artefacts they can use |

Default: contact the Managing Partner first; cc Practice Manager if findable.

## Source list (50-firm target)

Build from:
1. Law Institute of Victoria firm directory (filter by size band, suburb).
2. AUSTRAC reporting entity register (filter by Vic, ANZSIC code 6931 / legal services).
3. Doyle's Guide boutique listings (Property, Wills & Estates, Family).
4. LinkedIn search: "law firm" + Melbourne + 11–50 employees.
5. Word of mouth — referrals from existing professional network.

Track each firm in `outreach-tracker.csv`.
