I run ATTICUS — we build AML/CTF programs for independent Melbourne real estate agencies. Most of the agencies we speak to are in one of three positions on the new unified-program rules: enrolled and unsure what's next, partway through and stuck, or assuming it only applies to the major franchises.

Two questions:

1. Has {{AGENCY_NAME}} appointed an AMLCO and adopted a unified program?
2. Would a 12-point readiness checklist be useful before your next team meeting?

If yes to (2): {{CHECKLIST_URL}} — 8 minutes, no signup wall, you get a principal brief at the end.

There's no turnover or headcount threshold for real estate — every agency acting in a sale or purchase is captured. Buyer's agents in particular are exposed at the same level as a major-brand sales office.

{{SENDER_TITLE}} · ATTICUS

### Real-estate-specific objection — "We're too small for this to apply."

There's no size threshold in the Act for real estate. A two-agent office has the same statutory obligation as a 200-agent franchise — only the proportionality of controls differs. The good news is "proportionate" means small agencies don't need anything close to the full enterprise build; the readiness checklist will give you the minimum viable picture in 8 minutes: {{CHECKLIST_URL}}.
