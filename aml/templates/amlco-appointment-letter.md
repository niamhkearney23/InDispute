## Appointment as AML/CTF Compliance Officer

Dear {{AMLCO_FIRST_NAME}},

I am writing on behalf of the {{BOARD_OR_PARTNERSHIP}} to confirm your appointment as the AML/CTF Compliance Officer (AMLCO) of {{FIRM_LEGAL_NAME}} (ABN {{FIRM_ABN}}) under Part 7 of the *Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006* (Cth) and the AML/CTF Rules.

### 1. Authority

In this role you have the authority to:

- direct the day-to-day operation of the Firm's AML/CTF program;
- access all client files, accounting records and correspondence for the purpose of meeting our obligations under the Act;
- approve or decline the onboarding of high-risk clients;
- determine whether to lodge a suspicious matter report (SMR), threshold transaction report (TTR), or international funds transfer instruction (IFTI) report with AUSTRAC;
- direct the suspension or termination of any matter where you reasonably believe that continued provision of the designated service would contravene the Act;
- engage external specialists (legal, technology, training) as required, within an approved annual budget;
- communicate directly with AUSTRAC on behalf of the Firm.

### 2. Reporting line

You report on AML/CTF matters directly to the {{BOARD_OR_PARTNERSHIP}}, without filtering through any other partner or manager. You have a standing right to escalate matters to the {{BOARD_OR_PARTNERSHIP}} at any time.

You will provide a written AML/CTF compliance report to the {{BOARD_OR_PARTNERSHIP}} at least quarterly, covering, at a minimum:
- material risk-assessment changes;
- exception reporting (high-risk client decisions, EDD triggers);
- reports lodged with AUSTRAC;
- training completion rates;
- any communication with AUSTRAC;
- material breaches or near-misses.

### 3. Resources

The Firm will provide you with:
- sufficient time allocation to perform this role (currently estimated at {{HOURS_PER_WEEK}} hours per week);
- a discretionary annual AML/CTF budget of A${{BUDGET}};
- direct access to professional indemnity insurance, IT systems, and the Firm's professional advisers;
- backup AMLCO cover during periods of absence ({{BACKUP_AMLCO}}).

### 4. Independence

You may not be directed by any partner or employee to refuse to escalate, lodge or report a matter where you have formed (or hold information that would reasonably give rise to) a suspicion within the meaning of the Act. Any attempted interference must itself be escalated to the {{BOARD_OR_PARTNERSHIP}} and recorded.

### 5. Indemnity

The Firm indemnifies you against any liability arising from the proper performance of your duties under this appointment, on the terms set out in the Firm's indemnity policy at Annexure J. The indemnity does not extend to acts in bad faith or in conscious disregard of the Act.

### 6. Term

This appointment takes effect on {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} and continues until terminated by mutual agreement, your resignation, or your departure from the Firm. The Firm will provide reasonable notice of any contemplated change of AMLCO and will ensure an orderly handover.

### 7. Conditions of holding office

You confirm by signing below that you:
- are a partner, employee or contractor of the Firm with sufficient seniority to discharge the role;
- have read the Firm's AML/CTF program in full;
- have completed initial AML/CTF training and will complete annual refresher training;
- have disclosed any actual or potential conflicts of interest that may affect your performance of the role;
- are not currently the subject of any AUSTRAC, ASIC or law-society enforcement action that would compromise your ability to act.
