## What the Big 4 actually need

Each of your AML / financial crime practices was built for ADIs, super funds, and ASX-listed clients on six- and seven-figure engagements. Tranche 2 changes the inbound:

1. **Partners' personal networks are calling.** Family lawyer cousins, accountant friends, the real estate agent who sold the partner's beach house — all suddenly captured, none priceable at your standard rates.
2. **Existing audit and tax clients in the SME band are asking.** Compliance referrals come up in unrelated audit conversations, and there's nowhere clean to send them.
3. **Your professional-services-firm clients are asking *about their clients.*** Your big-firm law and accounting clients want to be able to refer their boutique peers and competitors somewhere.

The current options — "we can do this but it'll be $80k" or "we can't help, sorry" — both burn relationship capital. There's no good third answer.

## The proposed partnership

**Model:** "Preferred AML partner for SME referrals." Your firm refers in-scope SME clients you can't profitably engage. ATTICUS delivers. You retain the underlying client relationship for everything else (audit, tax, advisory).

**Why it works for you:**

- Your partners get to say *yes* to network referrals without dragging on your team.
- Zero delivery cost — referral economics, not subcontract.
- Brand-protected — ATTICUS is independent, not "powered by [Big 4]"; no risk of brand dilution from low-end work.
- Optionality on co-delivery for borderline matters that merit your name on the cover.
- Quarterly partner reporting on referred-client outcomes, so you can see relationship value.

**Why it works for us:**

- Distribution far beyond what cold outreach can produce.
- Trust transfer from your brand at the moment of referral.
- A path to co-branded artefacts on larger engagements where you choose to participate.

## Commercial structure (opening position)

Three options to discuss:

**A. Referral fee** — 10–15% of first-year fees on referred Tier 1 / Tier 2 engagements; nothing on Tier 3 (recurring) so the SME client isn't subsidising the channel forever.

**B. Marketing co-investment** — Your firm contributes brand and channel; ATTICUS contributes delivery and the IP. Net-zero exchange of value, structured as a memorandum of understanding rather than a fee.

**C. Hybrid** — Referral fee plus an optional co-delivery rate card where you choose to attach your name and a senior reviewer to the engagement (priced at your standard hourly rate plus an ATTICUS delivery margin).

We are open to whichever model fits your procurement and your client-facing comfort.

## Risk allocation

- **Professional indemnity:** ATTICUS carries primary PI for delivered work. Memorialised in the partnership agreement.
- **Independence:** ATTICUS is not a member of your network and is structured to remain independent. No conflict with your audit-side independence rules.
- **Confidentiality:** All referrals subject to standard NDA. We will not solicit your other clients off the back of a referred engagement.
- **Quality control:** You receive every Tier 2 / Tier 3 program document for review on request before client delivery, at no additional cost, for the first 12 months.
