Commercial structure

Three options to discuss.

A — Referral fee

Most common

10–15% of net fees on first-year Tier 1 / Tier 2 engagements. Nil on Tier 3 recurring fees, so the SME client isn't subsidising the channel forever. Quarterly payment in arrears.

B — Marketing co-investment

No fees in either direction. Each party contributes brand and channel; ATTICUS contributes delivery and IP. Structured as a memorandum of understanding.

C — Hybrid

Referral fee plus an optional co-delivery rate card where you choose to attach your name and a senior reviewer. Priced at your standard hourly rate plus a delivery margin.
