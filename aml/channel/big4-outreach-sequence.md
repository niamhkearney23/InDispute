# Big 4 — outreach sequence

**Goal:** secure a 45-minute introductory call with the Financial Crime / Regulatory practice partner at each of the four firms within 6 weeks.

**Cadence:** 3 touches over 18 days. Slower than direct-SME outreach — Big 4 partners read email differently.

**Voice:** peer-to-peer, specific, no SaaS-style "circling back" or "just bumping this." Reference their work.

---

## Reply playbook

| Reply pattern | Response |
|---|---|
| "Interested — book a time." | Confirm + send pitch doc as pre-read 24h before. Don't dump it now. |
| "Send more info first." | One paragraph + the pitch doc. End with: "If this lands, the next step is a 45-min call — happy to defer until you've read." |
| "We have an internal team that does this." | "Understood. Most firms in your tier do — the question is whether the unit economics work below ~$50k. If you ever hit a referral your team can't take, please come back." |
| "Speak to [more junior person]." | Take the introduction. Treat as Touch 1 with them; aim to be re-elevated. |
| "Not for us." | Thank them. Set a 12-month nurture (regulatory update notes). |
| "Could we white-label?" | "Yes — that's commercial option C in the pitch. Let's discuss on the call." |
| Out of office / no reply by touch 3 | Hold for 90 days, then re-approach with a case-study hook. |

---

## What to send when they ask for pre-reading

In order:
1. `big4-partnership-pitch.md` (rendered to PDF) — the headline pitch.
2. A redacted Tier 2 deliverable (one program document, anonymised).
3. Pricing summary + indicative referral economics.

Do NOT send the program template itself — it's the IP.

---

## Sequencing across the four firms

Don't fire all four touches simultaneously. Sequence by warmth:

1. **Week 1:** Whichever firm gives you a warm intro first.
2. **Week 2:** Next-warmest, plus the one with the most relevant recent Tranche 2 thought leadership.
3. **Week 3:** Remaining two.

Reason: if you sign one partnership exclusively or on a preferred basis, the others are dead conversations until renewal. Don't burn all four pipelines in parallel.
