# Big 4 — target contact map

> Names change. This document maps **roles** and **pathways to find the current incumbent**, not point-in-time names. Verify each contact via LinkedIn and the firm's public leadership page before reaching out.

## Roles to target at each firm

| Priority | Role / title pattern | Why them | Where they sit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Financial Crime / AML Practice Leader (Partner) | Owns the AML book; feels the SME-tier referral pain directly | Risk Consulting / Risk Advisory |
| **P1** | Regulatory & Compliance Partner (Financial Services) | Adjacent practice; often gets the inbound first | Risk Consulting / Financial Services |
| **P2** | Forensic / Financial Crime Director | Senior enough to champion internally; not the decision-maker | Forensic Services |
| **P2** | Private Clients Partner | Field inbound from HNW network — directly hits the SME law/accounting referral pattern | Private Clients / Private Wealth |
| **P3** | SME / Private Business Practice Leader | Owns the bulk of small-end audit and tax relationships; sees the inbound | Private Business / Mid-Market |
| **P3** | Innovation / Ventures / Alliances Lead | Sometimes the right entry for partnership-shaped conversations | Strategy / Innovation |

## Per-firm specifics

### PwC Australia
- AML / Financial Crime sits under **Risk Consulting**.
- Tip: PwC's response to recent regulator reviews (the firm has had public oversight) has elevated the **Regulatory & Compliance** practice — partners there are explicitly looking for ways to "extend reach without expanding fixed cost."
- Office focus for first conversation: Melbourne (Freshwater Place).

### EY Australia
- Look under **Financial Services Risk Management (FSRM)** and **Forensic & Integrity Services**.
- EY's SME / Private Tax practice is large and has the most direct line to inbound referrals.
- Office focus: Melbourne (Exhibition Street).

### Deloitte Australia
- AML sits under **Risk Advisory — Regulatory & Financial Crime**.
- Deloitte Private (the SME-facing arm) is the most likely first-conversation owner; Risk Advisory provides credibility.
- Office focus: Melbourne (Collins Square).

### KPMG Australia
- AML sits under **KPMG Forensic** and **Risk Consulting — Regulatory**.
- KPMG Enterprise is the SME-facing arm — strong inbound from the boutique tier.
- Office focus: Melbourne (Collins Street / 727 Collins).

## How to find the current incumbent (in order)

1. **Firm leadership page** — each firm publishes its partnership list with practice areas.
2. **LinkedIn search** — "Partner Financial Crime Australia [firm]" then filter by Melbourne.
3. **Recent thought leadership** — search the firm's insights page for "Tranche 2", "AML", "AUSTRAC", "designated non-financial businesses". Authors are reachable champions.
4. **AUSTRAC public submissions** — each firm filed responses to the Tranche 2 consultation. Named signatories = your warm leads.
5. **Conference circuit** — speakers at AFC Summit, ACAMS ANZ, AUSTRAC Innovation Hub events.
6. **Past job moves** — ex-AUSTRAC, ex-AFP, ex-ASIC staff at the Big 4 tend to be the AML champions.

## Warm-intro pathways (use before cold)

In priority order, look for warm intros via:

1. **Anyone in your direct network at any of the four firms** — even a senior manager can route to the right partner.
2. **Mid-tier alumni at Big 4** — partners who came up through BDO / GT / Pitcher and have peer relationships across.
3. **AFC / ACAMS chapter committees** — practitioner-to-practitioner.
4. **Boutique AML consultants** — peers, not competitors at the boutique end; happy to introduce upstream.
5. **Law firm partners with Big 4 audit relationships** — your existing direct-channel boutique-firm clients can introduce.

## What goes in the first outreach

- Reference to a specific piece of their recent thought leadership (Tranche 2 commentary, AUSTRAC submission, conference talk).
- One sentence stating the SME-referral gap.
- Specific ask: 45 minutes, two suggested times.
- Attachment: `big4-partnership-pitch.md` (rendered as PDF).

## What to avoid

- Pitching "we're cheaper than you" — wrong framing. The framing is "we extend your practice."
- Cold-pitching the Australia CEO or Risk Consulting Practice Leader — too senior; you'll get routed to a manager. Aim for partner level.
- Asking for a co-branded offering on first contact — that's a Year 2 conversation.
- Skipping the MoU step. Big 4 partnerships need paper before they're real.
