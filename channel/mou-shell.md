## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to record a non-exclusive working arrangement under which the Firm refers small and mid-sized reporting entities ("Referred Clients") to ATTICUS for AML/CTF program design, build, and where applicable ongoing AMLCO services, in circumstances where the Firm's own AML / financial crime practice does not propose to engage the Referred Client directly.

---

## 2. The reason for this arrangement

The Firm's AML / financial crime practice is structured to serve large reporting entities (ADIs, super funds, ASX-listed and equivalent). Following the Tranche 2 amendments to the AML/CTF Act commencing 1 July 2026, the Firm receives increasing inbound from small and mid-sized professional firms (legal, accounting, real estate) and other captured businesses for which the Firm's standard engagement structure is not commercially suited.

ATTICUS operates a productised AML/CTF compliance practice designed specifically for these entities, with three engagement tiers (DIY templates, Done-With-You, Outsourced AMLCO) and standard delivery within two weeks.

---

## 3. Scope of referrals

### 3.1 In scope
The Firm may, in its sole discretion, refer to ATTICUS any Referred Client who:
- is a reporting entity or prospective reporting entity under the AML/CTF Act;
- has annual revenue below {{REVENUE_CEILING}} (default: A$20m) or a fee-earner / staff headcount of fewer than {{HEADCOUNT_CEILING}} (default: 50); and
- has not been engaged by the Firm's AML / financial crime practice in the preceding 24 months on AML/CTF matters.

### 3.2 Out of scope
This MoU does not cover:
- existing clients of the Firm's AML / financial crime practice;
- regulated entities for whom the Firm has a specific engagement letter covering AML/CTF advice;
- entities where the Firm proposes to deliver the AML/CTF work itself.

## 4. Commercial structure

The parties elect option **{{OPTION_A_B_OR_C}}** below.

### Option A — Referral fee
ATTICUS pays the Firm a referral fee equal to:
- **{{REFERRAL_PCT}}%** (default: 12%) of net fees invoiced and collected from the Referred Client in the first 12 months of engagement on Tier 1 (DIY Templates) and Tier 2 (Done-With-You);
- **nil** on Tier 3 (Outsourced AMLCO) recurring fees, to keep the SME client from subsidising the referral fee indefinitely.

Referral fees are paid quarterly in arrears, within 30 days of the end of each calendar quarter, against an invoice from the Firm. Referral fees include GST.

### Option B — Marketing co-investment (no fee)
No fees are payable in either direction. Each party contributes brand and channel; ATTICUS contributes delivery and intellectual property. The parties agree to mutual referrals: the Firm refers in-scope Referred Clients to ATTICUS, and ATTICUS refers up-market reporting entities ($20m+ revenue) to the Firm where appropriate.

### Option C — Hybrid
Referral fee per Option A, plus an optional co-delivery rate card under which the Firm may attach its name and a designated senior reviewer to specific engagements. Co-delivered engagements are priced at the Firm's standard hourly rate plus an ATTICUS delivery margin of **{{HYBRID_MARGIN}}%** (default: 25%). The rate card is at Annexure B.

## 5. Referral mechanics

### 5.1 Referral handoff
The Firm nominates {{REFERRAL_CONCIERGE_TITLE}} (default: a senior manager in the Risk Consulting practice) as the single point of referral handoff. Referrals are made by email to {{REFERRAL_EMAIL}}, with a copy of the Firm's introduction to the Referred Client.

### 5.2 Acknowledgement
ATTICUS acknowledges each referral within one business day and provides a status update within five business days of initial Referred Client contact (engaged / scoping / declined / no contact).

### 5.3 Quarterly reporting
ATTICUS provides a quarterly written report to the Firm covering:
- number of referrals received;
- conversion to engagement;
- engagement tier mix;
- aggregate fees invoiced and collected;
- referral fees payable (Option A or C).

The report is provided in non-attributed form to preserve Referred Client confidentiality; individual Referred Client identification is provided only with the Referred Client's prior written consent.

## 6. Confidentiality (binding)

Confidentiality obligations survive termination for three years. Standard exceptions apply (publicly available information; information independently developed; required disclosure under law).

---

## 7. Independence and conflicts (binding)

### 7.1 Independence
ATTICUS is not a member of the Firm's network and is not subject to the Firm's independence regime. Nothing in this MoU is intended to compromise the Firm's auditor independence or to create any party-to-party representation.

### 7.2 No agency
Neither party is the agent of the other. ATTICUS does not act in the Firm's name; the Firm does not warrant or represent ATTICUS's services.

### 7.3 Conflict checks
The Firm runs its standard conflict check before making a referral. ATTICUS runs its own client conflict check before accepting an engagement.

### 7.4 Non-solicitation
ATTICUS will not, during the term of this MoU and for 12 months after termination, solicit the Firm's clients for services other than AML/CTF compliance work, except where ATTICUS independently establishes the contact without reference to information obtained under this MoU.

---

## 8. Data and privacy (binding)

ATTICUS is a separate APP entity under the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth). All Referred Client personal information is collected, held and used by ATTICUS under its own privacy policy. The Firm transfers personal information to ATTICUS only with Referred Client consent or other lawful basis.

---

## 9. Quality assurance

For the first 12 months from the effective date, the Firm may request the Tier 2 / Tier 3 program document for any Referred Client engagement and review it before client delivery, at no cost. ATTICUS will incorporate the Firm's reasonable comments on a best-efforts basis. The Firm does not warrant or take responsibility for ATTICUS's work product.

ATTICUS carries professional indemnity insurance to a minimum of A$2m per claim and in aggregate, with the Firm noted as an interested party on request.

---

## 10. Term and termination (binding)

This MoU may be terminated:
- by either party for any reason on 60 days' written notice;
- by either party immediately for material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice;
- by either party immediately on the insolvency of the other.

On termination:
- the Firm stops making new referrals;
- ATTICUS completes any in-flight Referred Client engagements;
- referral fees accrued and payable under Option A or C remain payable;
- the confidentiality, independence, data, and non-solicit obligations continue under their stated terms.
