Worth knowing

"Complimentary" doesn't mean free of cost.

InfoTrack's pricing line is "no charge for the software, you only pay for searches." The software is genuinely free. The searches are not optional — every client onboarding requires a VOI, PEP and sanctions screening at minimum, and most matters require adverse-media and beneficial-owner checks too.

The free-until-31-May-2026 window applies only to personnel onboarding — verifying your firm's own staff. Client CDD checks have always been chargeable. After 31 May 2026, even personnel checks revert to pay-per-use.

For a small firm doing ~200 client onboardings a year and verifying ~10 internal staff, you're looking at a real annual cost somewhere between $2,000 and $6,000 depending on check mix. That's still cheaper than building it in-house — and the platform's workflow integration is genuinely valuable. But "free" is marketing copy, not the actual unit economics.

None of which is a criticism. InfoTrack's product is well-built and well-priced for what it does. We just think the cost conversation should be honest about per-check exposure, and the scope conversation should be honest about what software can and can't do.

Decision shortcut

Three questions, in order.

Question 1

Are you in law, conveyancing, accounting or real estate?

No — InfoTrack doesn't cover your sector. You need a real program, and almost certainly an external AMLCO. See our sector pages: TCSPs, DPMS, gambling, MSB, crypto, bullion.

Yes — continue to Q2.

Question 2

Do you have a partner who can credibly hold the AMLCO role — time, AML knowledge, comfort with personal exposure?

Yes — InfoTrack's platform plus that partner is likely sufficient for low–medium risk work. You'll still need an independent review at intervals; that part can't sit with InfoTrack.

No / unsure — continue to Q3.

Question 3

Does your work touch international clients, complex trust structures, high-value real estate, or repeat high-cash transactions?

Yes — your risk profile is meaningfully above what an internal nominal-AMLCO can defend. ATTICUS's outsourced AMLCO retainer is built for exactly this.

No — supplement InfoTrack with us only for the independent review (mandatory regardless).
