## Confirmed facts

- Strandline Resources Limited and its subsidiary Coburn Resources Pty Ltd operated a mineral sands project together.
- Both entered receivership and voluntary administration on the same day (21 February 2025); Robert Brauer, Jason Preston and Linda Smith of McGrathNicol were appointed.
- 166 workers had written employment contracts naming Strandline, the parent, as employer, despite working on Coburn's project.
- Unpaid employee entitlements carry priority in an insolvency. The Commonwealth, having paid those entitlements via the fair entitlements guarantee scheme and standing in the workers' shoes by subrogation, sought to have that priority attach to Coburn, where more asset value sat, ahead of Coburn's secured lenders.
- Jackson J held the written contracts naming Strandline as employer ordinarily prevail; an "intelligible business objective" for the group's employment structure is a relevant consideration but not a standalone test.
- Effect: the Commonwealth's priority claim attaches to Strandline, not Coburn. Coburn's secured lenders are not leapfrogged.

## Learning line

Learn this: in a group insolvency, the written employment contract is the starting point for identifying the true employer, not which company's project the work was actually done for.
