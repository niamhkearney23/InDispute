# Teleprompter, Farrugia. Words only.

No stage directions, no timings. Line breaks are breath points, so pause where the line ends. Blank line means a proper beat.

Two versions. The long one is what you already have, timed honestly. The short one actually lands on 60.

---

# LONG VERSION, about 90 seconds

Imagine your own barrister stands up in court,
and tells the judge that you are the worse criminal.

That happened.
And last week the High Court said it was not enough to overturn the sentence.

Two men.
Both pleaded guilty to conspiring to traffic a commercial quantity of drugs.
And the same barrister turned up to represent both of them at the same sentencing hearing.

Which, by the way, is allowed.
There is no rule against it.

The prosecution filed written submissions saying the other man should get the heavier sentence.
More drugs, more transactions, longer record.

Their shared barrister disagreed.
He told the judge his own client was worse.

The judge actually stopped him and checked.

"You agree, though, that Mr Kanmaz is more culpable than Mr Farrugia, don't you?"

"No, I say the opposite."

Eleven years.
The other man got nine.

So he appealed, and said his barrister had been stuck between two clients.
And he lost.
Because he never put on any evidence of what he was actually advised, or what he instructed.
He kept privilege over all of it.

And the person alleging the error is the person who has to prove it.
He couldn't.
So the sentence stands.

## The three lines that carry it

If you are tired and want to stop rehearsing, rehearse these and let the rest be conversational.

1. **"tells the judge that you are the worse criminal"** — the hook. Flat, not dramatic.
2. **"No, I say the opposite."** — the screenshot. Give it its own beat on both sides.
3. **"if you allege the error, you prove it"** — the landing. Slow down into it.

## Still true, do not drift off these

- He **lost**. Not overturned, not reduced.
- Nobody found the barrister did anything wrong. "He couldn't prove it", never "his barrister was conflicted".
