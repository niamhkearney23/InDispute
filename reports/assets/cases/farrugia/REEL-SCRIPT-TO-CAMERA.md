# To-camera script, Farrugia v The King [2026] HCA 28

**Why this one of the three.** Ko is the better law. S32 is the better tutorial. Farrugia is the better *story*, and this is a talking video, so story wins. It has a hook a non-lawyer gets in one sentence, a real courtroom exchange you can perform, a twist, and a lesson. You do not have to explain a single piece of legislation to land it.

**Format:** one shot, straight to camera, no cuts needed. **About 90 seconds** at a natural pace, not 60. A 30 second cutdown is at the bottom, and a true 60 second version is in `TELEPROMPTER.md` alongside a words-only read of this one.

---

## ⚠️ TWO THINGS YOU CANNOT MISSTATE ON CAMERA

Video is harder to correct than a caption, so read these twice before you record.

1. **He lost. The appeal was dismissed.** Do not say "he won", "it was overturned", or "he got it reduced". He did not.
2. **Nobody found the barrister did anything wrong.** The High Court found the opposite: on the evidence, no conflict was proved. Say "he could not prove it", never "his barrister was conflicted" as a statement of fact. The script below is already worded safely. If you improvise, keep that line.

---

## THE SCRIPT, 60 seconds

Read the bold as the line. The notes in brackets are for you, not for saying.

---

**[0:00 — Hook. Straight in, no "hi guys". Say it flat, like you are telling a friend something unbelievable.]**

**Imagine your own barrister stands up in court, and tells the judge that you are the worse criminal.**

**[0:05 — Beat. Let that sit for one second.]**

**That happened. And last week the High Court said it was not enough to overturn the sentence.**

**[0:11 — Now the setup. Slightly faster, you are laying track.]**

**Two men. Both pleaded guilty to conspiring to traffic a commercial quantity of drugs. And the same barrister turned up to represent both of them at the same sentencing hearing.**

**[0:20 — Small shrug on this line. It is the surprising bit.]**

**Which, by the way, is allowed. There is no rule against it.**

**[0:24 — The turn. Slow down here.]**

**The prosecution filed written submissions saying the other man should get the heavier sentence. More drugs, more transactions, longer record.**

**[0:32 — Lean in.]**

**Their shared barrister disagreed. He told the judge his own client was worse.**

**[0:37 — Perform this. Two voices, or just a tilt of the head between them. This is the moment people screenshot.]**

**The judge actually stopped him and checked. "You agree, though, that Mr Kanmaz is more culpable than Mr Farrugia, don't you?"**

**"No, I say the opposite."**

**[0:45 — Flat delivery. Let the numbers do it.]**

**Eleven years. The other man got nine.**

**[0:49 — The appeal.]**

**So he appealed, and said his barrister had been stuck between two clients. And he lost. Because he never put on any evidence of what he was actually advised, or what he instructed. He kept privilege over all of it.**

**[0:59 — The lesson. This is the line to land. Slight pause before it.]**

**And the person alleging the error is the person who has to prove it. He couldn't. So the sentence stands.**

**[1:04 — Optional tag if you want to push the comments. Drop it if you want to end clean.]**

**The Court did say that if he had proved it, it would have mattered.**

Keep it minimal, one line at a time, cream text on a dark strip or just plain white with a shadow.

| 0:20 | this is allowed |

| 0:45 | 11 years / 9 years |
| 0:59 | you have to prove it |
| End card | Farrugia v The King [2026] HCA 28 |

**Do not** put the case citation on screen at the start. It reads as homework and people swipe. Put it at the end, once they already care.

---

## DELIVERY NOTES

- **Do not open with a greeting.** The first three seconds decide it. "Imagine your own barrister" is the whole hook, so it has to be word one.
- **Sit still.** The story is doing the work. Hands moving a lot makes it feel like an explainer.
- **The transcript line is the performance.** Everything before it is setup and everything after is payoff. If you only rehearse one bit, rehearse that.
- **Do not smile through the numbers.** Eleven years is a real person's sentence.
- Say **"co-offender"**, not "co-accused". They had already pleaded guilty.
- If you fluff a line, keep rolling and say it again. Cut in the edit.

## CAPTION FOR THE REEL

Short. The video is the content, the caption is not a second essay.

Your own barrister, telling the judge you are the worse of the two.

He appealed on conflict of interest and lost, because he never put on evidence of what he was advised or what he instructed. The person alleging the error is the one who has to prove it.

Eleven years stands.
