## The "own your own firm" angle, handled carefully

We cannot promise anyone will own a firm, and the Bar's publicity rules bar overstated claims. What is true and usable:

> Every advocate and solicitor in Peninsular Malaysia, including every firm owner, started with nine months of pupillage.

The campaign frames pupillage at a disputes-only firm as the first step of that path: you see the whole life of a matter, from letter of demand to trial, in a firm small enough that you are in the room. That's the standing it gives them, which fits our "gain, never shortfall" rule. No "it's hard to find a place" framing.

Any requirements before a lawyer can open their own firm (years of practice, Bar Council rules on sole proprietorships) go in **only after checking the current Bar Council rules**. Leave them out until then.
