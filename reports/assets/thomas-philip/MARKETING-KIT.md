## 2. Guardrails: Legal Profession (Publicity) Rules 2001

Malaysian advocates may publicise, within limits. Drafted to stay inside them:

- No superlatives or comparisons with other firms ("leading", "best", "largest", "top").
- No claims about success rates, wins, or results in past matters.
- No client names or matters without written client consent; nothing that could identify a live dispute.
- No testimonials from clients. (Same as our own rule: never invented.)
- No fee undercutting or "cheapest" framing.
- Factual, dignified, verifiable. Our house style (restraint, short declaratives) already fits.

Firm should run each piece past whoever handles Bar compliance. This is a drafting aid, not advice on the Rules.
