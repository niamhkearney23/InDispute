## Option 1, the funny one (recommended)

We are hiring interns, and we promise you will do more than photocopy things.

Here is the honest job description:

You will read judgments before the partners do. You will summarise them in plain English, which is harder than it sounds and more useful than anything you learned drafting a memo nobody read. You will pitch which cases matter and defend the pitch. You will watch legal content get made, and you will make some of it.

You will not: fetch coffee, sit silently in meetings, or spend three months on a discovery task that a machine could do.

If you are the person in your cohort who explains the case to everyone else the night before the exam, this is for you.
