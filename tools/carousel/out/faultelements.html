One offence. Three elements. Three different states of mind.
Importing a commercial quantity of a border controlled drug, section 307.1(1) of the Criminal Code.
s 307.1(1)(a)
The person imports a substance.
Intention
He meant to import it.
s 307.1(1)(b)
The substance is a border controlled drug.
Recklessness
He saw the risk and ran it.
s 307.1(1)(c)
The quantity is a commercial quantity.
Absolute liability
No state of mind required.
An offence does not have one fault element. Each physical element has its own.
Elements and fault elements as set out in The King v Ko [2026] HCA 29 at [12] to [13]. General information, not legal advice.
