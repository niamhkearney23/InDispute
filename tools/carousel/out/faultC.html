Three elements. Three states of mind.
s 307.1(1)(a)
Intention
That he imports a substance. He meant to.
s 307.1(1)(b)
Recklessness
That it is a border controlled drug. He saw the risk and ran it.
s 307.1(1)(c)
Absolute liability
That the quantity is commercial. No state of mind at all.
