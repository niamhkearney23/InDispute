# ICP — Boutique Melbourne accounting firms (AML/CTF)

## Why this ICP

Accounting firms doing TCSP-style work — company / trust formation, SMSF establishment, acting as nominee director / trustee, providing registered office services, managing client money — are pulled into Tranche 2 in the same way as lawyers, but with less external prompting from professional bodies than lawyers received. Many principals don't yet realise they're in scope.

## Hard filters

| Filter | Target |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Melbourne metropolitan (CBD spine, inner east, inner south, inner north, eastern suburbs cluster) |
| **Headcount** | 3–25 fee-earners (excludes solo, excludes mid-tier and above) |
| **Service mix** | At least one of: business advisory, structuring, SMSF administration, trust accounting, "office of company secretary" style service |
| **Client money** | Operates a client trust account or similar |
| **Independence** | Not a member of a national network (e.g. BDO, Findex, Pitcher Partners) |
| **AUSTRAC enrolment** | Public register check — segment (a) enrolled, (b) not enrolled (hotter) |

## Soft signals (prioritise)

- Website mentions "structuring", "asset protection", "family office", "SMSF" → designated-service exposure is real.
- Active SMSF Association / IPA / SMSF specialist accreditation → likely doing fund establishment.
- Offers "company formation" or "trust establishment" as a productised service → clear TCSP work.
- No mention of AML/CTF / compliance on website → under-prepared.
- Recently took on a structuring or business-advisory partner → triggers internal review.

## Disqualifiers

- Pure tax compliance / BAS shops with no structuring or TCSP work — they may genuinely be out of scope.
- Recently announced acquisition by a national network.
- Already publish an AML/CTF program — rare; means in-house capability.

## Personas

| Persona | Title patterns | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Managing Principal** | Principal, Director, Managing Partner | Risk framing — principal liability, AUSTRAC enrolment |
| **Practice Manager / GM** | Practice Manager, General Manager | Operational framing — intake workflow, training |
| **SMSF / Advisory Lead** | SMSF Manager, Structuring Specialist | Peer framing — concrete worked examples |
