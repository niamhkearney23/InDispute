# ICP — Independent Melbourne real estate agencies (AML/CTF)

## Why this ICP

Real estate is the highest-volume capture in Tranche 2. There is no turnover or headcount threshold — every agency acting in a sale or purchase is in scope. Boutique independents have the same obligation as Ray White or Belle and significantly less head-office support to lean on.

## Hard filters

| Filter | Target |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Melbourne metropolitan — focus on high-value postcodes (Toorak, Brighton, Kew, Hawthorn, South Yarra, Carlton, East Melbourne, Albert Park) plus inner suburbs |
| **Type** | Independent agency (NOT a franchise of Ray White, Belle, Jellis Craig, McGrath, etc.) — franchise model usually means head-office program |
| **Headcount** | 2–25 sales agents |
| **Service mix** | Residential or commercial sales, buyer's agency, off-the-plan / developer sales |
| **Trust account** | Operates a sales trust account |
| **AUSTRAC enrolment** | Public register check |

## Soft signals (prioritise)

- High average sale price (>$2M) → larger AML risk per matter, principal will care.
- Active buyer's agent service → highest CDD obligation per matter.
- Foreign-buyer or international marketing language on website → likely cross-border exposure.
- Off-the-plan / developer marketing → multiple high-value transactions, opaque purchaser structures.
- No AML/CTF or compliance mention on website → under-prepared.

## Disqualifiers

- Franchise office of a national brand (head office handles).
- Property management only (no sales) — usually out of scope.
- Recently sold to or merged with a major brand.

## Personas

| Persona | Title patterns | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Principal / Director** | Director, Principal, Licensee | Risk framing — principal/licensee accountability under the Act |
| **Office Manager** | Office Manager, Operations Manager | Operational framing — intake into the CRM, agent training |
| **Buyer's Agent (sole) / Sales Manager** | Buyer's Agent, Sales Manager | Peer framing — what good looks like at similar agencies |
