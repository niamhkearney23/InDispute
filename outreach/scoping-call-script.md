# 20-minute scoping call script

**Goal:** end the call with a tier recommendation, a written quote sent within 24h, and a follow-up date in the calendar.

## 0:00 — Frame (1 min)
"Thanks for the time. Plan for the next 20 minutes: 5 minutes on your firm and where you are with AML, 10 minutes on what good looks like for a firm your size, 5 minutes on next steps and pricing. Sound right?"

## 1:00 — Discovery (7 min)
- Practice areas — which generate designated services?
- Headcount: partners, lawyers, paralegals, admin
- Trust account turnover and matter volume
- Current state: AUSTRAC enrolled? AMLCO appointed? Program drafted?
- Internal capacity to drive this — who, how many hours/week?
- Deadline awareness — comfort level on 1 July
- Any AUSTRAC contact to date?

## 8:00 — Diagnose & frame the gap (5 min)
- Reflect their position back in one sentence.
- Map to one of three patterns: "haven't started," "drafting and stuck," "operating but unsure if defensible."
- Name the biggest risk for their pattern (usually: AMLCO appointed without supporting program, or CDD outside intake workflow).

## 13:00 — Recommend tier (4 min)
- Match recommendation to honest internal capacity assessment.
- Be willing to recommend Tier 1 if they genuinely have capacity. Builds trust.
- Be direct if Tier 1 is unrealistic given timeline: "I wouldn't recommend templates from this starting point — you'd run out of runway."

## 17:00 — Next steps (3 min)
- Confirm: written quote within 24h, scoped to their answers.
- Book the next call (kickoff if proceeding, decision check-in if considering).
- Send: thank you email + quote + landing page link + checklist link.

## Common objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We'll wait and see if AUSTRAC enforces it." | "Reasonable instinct, but the AMLCO obligation is personal. Even without enforcement, partners are exposed if a matter goes sideways." |
| "It's cheaper to do it in-house." | "Often true on the build, rarely on the maintain. The 12-month run cost is what catches people out." |
| "Our accountant is doing it." | "Common — and fine if the accountant has AML/CTF expertise. Worth checking they're covering the program elements specific to legal designated services." |
| "We're below the threshold." | "There isn't a turnover threshold for legal designated services. The trigger is providing the service at all." |
| "Send a proposal and I'll consider." | "Happy to. Two questions first so the proposal is actually useful: [discovery questions]." |
